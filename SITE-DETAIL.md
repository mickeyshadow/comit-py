# Site-level fuel use and technology change

**v2 universe - all UK industry**, anchored to the DESNZ territorial
industry total (~46.5 Mt in 2025), in five transparent layers: the
top-100 ETS installations named, the ETS tail aggregated, NAEI
non-traded point sources >=10 kt named, their tail aggregated, and a
diffuse remainder. The hindcast (skill score) stays defined on the
ETS-verified core, where annual outturn exists.

Transition dates carry their range across the seven ensemble
worlds; a date appearing in few worlds is a fragile (near-tie)
event, not a forecast.
